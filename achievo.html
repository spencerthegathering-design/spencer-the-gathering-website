// THE IDEA

An App You're Encouraged To Close.

Most social apps measure their value in minutes on screen. Achievo measures its value in the things you go and do after you close it.

You set the quests yourself: a daily run, a hydration target, an hour off your phone, the book you keep meaning to finish, the call to your mum that keeps slipping. The app gives you camera-proof posts, XP, streaks that pause when life pauses, and a small circle of people who notice when you turn up. It celebrates the moment, then steps out of your way. There's no infinite scroll at the bottom of an Achievo session. There's a life there, waiting to be lived.

// WHAT'S INSIDE

Six Things It Does Differently.

Built deliberately as the inverse of every doom-scroll app you've ever closed by accident at one in the morning.

📹

A Dual Feed

Vertical video on one side, threaded conversation on the other. Enough video to spark something — and a place to actually talk about it with the people who saw it too.

🏆

Quests & XP

Runs, hydration, focus sessions, journalling, the gym, the cooking, the calling-your-mum. Set the quests that fit your life. The XP and levels follow on quietly behind.

🔥

Streaks That Pause For Life

Built to reward consistency, not perfection. If you skip a day because life happened, your streak pauses rather than breaks. Sustainable on purpose.

📸

Camera-Proof Posts

Built-in timers and beeps link each photo and video to the quest it completes. The receipts are real, which is what makes the wins feel earned.

♿

Designed For Everyone

Wheelchair users, neurodivergent minds, low vision and Deaf players, people with chronic fatigue — designed for from the first sketch, not retrofitted. The deep-dive is further down.

👥

A Small, Real Community

Local leaderboards, friendship lines that light up when both of you are on a streak, and your thirteen squad pets along for the ride. Tight, real, and built for two or more.

// EVERYONE WELCOME

Built Accessibility-First.

Not a settings menu we tack on at the end. Every design decision starts with one question — can everyone use this? When the answer is no, the design changes. Not the user.

🦽

Motor & Wheelchair

One-hand reach zones, large touch targets, no time-pressured taps, every gesture swappable for a single tap, and full external switch support.

🧠

Neurodivergent

Reduce-motion built in. A calm-feed mode that drops the visual noise. ADHD-friendly time blocks. Predictable layouts. No surprise sounds, no surprise popups.

👁️

Low Vision & Blind

Full VoiceOver and TalkBack support. Dynamic text scaling that doesn't break layout. High-contrast themes. No information conveyed by colour alone. Real image descriptions, not afterthoughts.

👂

Deaf & Hard Of Hearing

Auto and manual captions on every video. No audio-only cues anywhere. Vibration alternatives for sound. ASL and Auslan creators highlighted in the discovery feed.

🛌

Chronic Illness & Fatigue

Streaks pause when you do. Quest difficulty scales to your week. There are no guilt notifications — rest is a feature, not a failure state.

🌍

Language & Reading

Plain language by default. A dyslexia-friendly font option. Voice-to-post for anyone who'd rather not type. Full translation at launch.

// FAQ

Things People Ask.

Anything missing? Email spencerthegathering@gmail.com.

When does Achievo launch?
Closed beta opens in late 2026. Public launch follows in 2027. The waitlist sets the order of beta invites.
Is Achievo really free?
Yes. The free tier includes the dual feed, every quest template, streaks, XP, camera-proof posts and the full accessibility suite. It's supported by ads between feed posts. Achievo Pro removes the ads and adds a gold PRO ★ next to your name — final pricing will be set close to launch, likely a few dollars a month.
What does the Pro badge actually look like?
A subtle gold star next to your name wherever it appears — profile, feed posts, leaderboards, quest comments. Not flashy. A quiet thank-you visible to the people you interact with.
How is this different from Instagram or TikTok?
Three differences worth naming. One: the app is built around quests you set yourself, not an algorithm's idea of what'll keep you scrolling. Two: streaks pause when life pauses, instead of punishing rest. Three: every feature is designed for accessibility from the first sketch, not patched on at the end.
Will my data be sold to advertisers?
No. The free tier shows ads from a privacy-respecting network, but no personal data is sold or fed into ad targeting. Read the full privacy policy for the detail.
Which platforms?
iOS and Android at launch, tablets included. Web and desktop are on the longer roadmap.
What if I have a disability you haven't listed?
Tell us. Email Spencerthegathering@gmail.com with your needs and we'll design for them. Accessibility-first is a conversation, not a fixed checklist.
