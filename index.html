// THE STUDIO

Stuff That Earns Its Place.

We measure success in the minutes you spend off your phone, not on it. A good round of Quantum Button is done in under a minute, leaves you grinning, and hands you back to your life. That's the whole idea.

Somewhere along the way, apps stopped being built for people and started being built to keep them scrolling. We got sick of it. So everything we make has one rule: it has to earn its place on your phone. No dark patterns, no tricks, no growth targets we've got to hit by Tuesday. There are no shareholders to keep happy and no roadmap we didn't write ourselves — just us, thirteen pets, and the next thing we're proud enough of to ship.

// HOUSE RULES

Four Pillars.

Four rules we wrote down before a single line of code, and hold every build to. The day one of them bends to a deadline is the day it doesn't ship.

⚡

Built To Be Finished

A good game leaves you wanting another round. A great one lets you walk away from it. Short sessions. Clean endings. Joy you don't have to fight your way out of.

👥

Made For Humans. Plural.

Every feature is built to bring two or more people together. Solo modes are the exception, never the default. No engagement bots, no synthetic hearts, no friend suggestions you never asked for.

♿

Accessibility, From The First Line

Not a settings menu we'll add later. Wheelchair users, neurodivergent minds, the Deaf community, low vision and blind players, and people managing chronic fatigue get the full app — never a watered-down version of it.

🐕

Built Like It Matters

Just us. Thirteen cats and dogs supervising, mostly by sleeping. No shareholders pushing for engagement, no roadmap we didn't write ourselves, no growth target waiting on Tuesday. Just the next version, and whether it's actually ready.

// FAQ

Common Questions.

If yours isn't here, email hello@spencer-the-gathering.com — a real person reads it.

When does Quantum Button launch?
This week. It goes public now — play right in your browser or grab it on Android via Google Play, with iOS to follow. Leave your email and we'll send you the link the moment it's live.
How does the live video feed actually work?
Peer-to-peer, over an encrypted WebRTC connection. The stream goes straight between the two devices in a match — it never touches our servers and nothing is recorded. When the round ends, the connection closes and the video is gone.
Is Achievo free?
Yes. The free tier includes every quest template, all streaks, the dual feed, camera-proof posts and the full accessibility suite. It's supported by ads between feed posts. Achievo Pro removes the ads and adds a gold PRO ★ next to your name across the app. Pro will be a few dollars a month at launch — final pricing is set closer to release.
Are the cats and dogs actually real?
All thirteen. Each in-game avatar is modelled on a real cat or dog who shares our studio. The photos are real, the names are real, and the chewed cables are very real.
Do you work with brands or other studios?
Not actively — both apps take all the time we've got. But a good partnership is always worth a chat. Send us an email and tell us what you're thinking.
Which platforms will the apps be on?
Quantum Button launches this week in your browser and on Android (Google Play) — no download needed to jump in. iOS follows once it's up and earning its keep. Achievo will take a similar path when it arrives.
