// THE STUDIO

We Build Things Worth Coming Back To.

The internet is full of stuff that steals your time. We're building the opposite — software with a heartbeat, a sense of humour, and a reason to put your phone down when you're done.

Spencer The Gathering is a one-person studio with a thirteen-strong support team of cats and dogs. We make weird, vibrant, fiercely social experiences — built from scratch, refined in our project folder until they sing, and shipped only when they're ready to make someone genuinely happy. No dark patterns. No mystery loot box psychology. No "engagement metrics" that secretly mean "addiction". Just real games, real connection, and real fun.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions? We've Got Answers.

The stuff people ask us most. If your question isn't here, hit us up at spencerthegathering@gmail.com.

When is Quantum Button actually launching?
The closed beta is happening in early 2026 with public launch targeted later that year. Join the waitlist and you'll be one of the first invited in. We'd rather ship a small thing that's ready than a big thing that isn't.
How does the live video feed in Quantum Button work?
It's true peer-to-peer — the video stream goes directly between you and your opponent over a secure WebRTC connection. There is no server in the middle watching, recording or storing your face. Only the two of you ever see each other.
Will Achievo be free?
Yes. Achievo Free is free to download and free to use forever — feed, quests, streaks, friends and every accessibility feature are all included. It's ad-supported. If you'd rather an ad-free experience, Achievo Pro removes all ads and adds a glowing PRO ★ symbol next to your name across the app, so your squad knows you're keeping the lights on.
Are the cats and dogs really real?
Yes. All thirteen. Every avatar in our games is a real, live, beloved member of the squad — cats and dogs alike. We have the photos. We have the receipts. We have the chew toys and the scratching posts.
Do you work with brands or other studios?
We're heads-down on Quantum Button and Achievo right now, but we're always open to interesting conversations. Drop us an email and tell us what you're thinking.
Which platforms will the apps be on?
Both Quantum Button and Achievo are launching on iOS and Android first. Desktop and web versions are on the longer-term roadmap.
