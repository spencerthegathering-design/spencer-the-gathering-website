// THE STUDIO

Software That Earns Its Place.

We measure success in minutes off-screen, not on. A good round of Quantum Button ends in under a minute and leaves you grinning. A good day on Achievo ends with you logged off — and the run you set as a quest actually done.

Somewhere around 2012, software stopped being built for people. The apps that pulled it off weren't built by villains; they were built by smart engineers optimising for the wrong number. We're trying to optimise for the right ones. Joy. Connection. A run, finished. A friend, called. There are no shareholders pushing for "engagement", and no targets that have to be hit by Tuesday. There is one builder, thirteen pets, and the next version of each app sitting in a project folder until it earns the right to be shipped.

ACHIEVO

A social app that wants you to log off.

Vertical video on one side. Threaded conversations on the other. Set the quests that matter — drink water, run five kilometres, finish the book — and the app turns each one into XP, streaks, and a small circle of people who notice when you turn up. Built accessibility-first from the first sketch: designed for wheelchair users, neurodivergent minds, low vision and Deaf players, and anyone the internet routinely forgets.

✓A dual feed: video to inspire you, threads to talk it over
✓Daily quests for runs, hydration, focus, journaling, the lot
✓Streaks that pause when life happens, instead of punishing rest
✓Camera-proof posts with built-in timers — the receipts are real
✓Accessibility designed first, not bolted on at the end
✓Free with ads, or Achievo Pro ★ for an ad-free feed and a gold badge by your name

// HOUSE RULES

Four Pillars.

Four rules we wrote down before a line of code, and check every build against. The day a pillar bends to a deadline is the day the build doesn't ship.

⚡

Built To Be Finished

A good game leaves you wanting another round. A great one lets you walk away from it. Short sessions. Clean endings. Joy you don't have to fight your way out of.

👥

Made For Humans. Plural.

Every feature is built to bring two or more people together. Solo modes are the exception, never the default. No engagement bots, no synthetic hearts, no friend suggestions you never asked for.

♿

Accessibility, From The First Line

Not a settings menu we'll add later. Wheelchair users, neurodivergent minds, the Deaf community, low vision and blind players, and people managing chronic fatigue get the full app — never a watered-down version of it.

🐕

Built Like It Matters

One builder. Thirteen cats and dogs supervising, mostly by sleeping. There are no shareholders pushing for engagement, no roadmap we didn't write ourselves, and no growth target waiting on Tuesday. Just the next version, and the question of whether it's ready.

// FAQ

Common Questions.

If yours isn't here, email spencerthegathering@gmail.com — a real person reads it.

When does Quantum Button launch?
Closed beta runs through the first half of 2026, with the public launch later that same year. The waitlist sets the order of beta invites. We'd rather release something small that works than something big that doesn't, so the dates may breathe a little.
How does the live video feed actually work?
Peer-to-peer, over an encrypted WebRTC connection. The stream goes straight between the two devices in a match — it never touches our servers and nothing is recorded. When the round ends, the connection closes and the video is gone.
Is Achievo free?
Yes. The free tier includes every quest template, all streaks, the dual feed, camera-proof posts and the full accessibility suite. It's supported by ads between feed posts. Achievo Pro removes the ads and adds a gold PRO ★ next to your name across the app. Pro will be a few dollars a month at launch — final pricing is set closer to release.
Are the cats and dogs actually real?
All thirteen. Each in-game avatar is modelled on a real cat or dog who shares the studio. The photos are real, the names are real, and the chewed cables are very real.
Do you work with brands or other studios?
Not actively — both apps take all the time we have. But interesting partnerships are always worth a conversation. Send an email and tell us what you're thinking.
Which platforms will the apps be on?
iOS and Android at launch for both apps. A web build is on the longer roadmap, not the next twelve months.
