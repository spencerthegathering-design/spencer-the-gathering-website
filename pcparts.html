// THE IDEA

One Question, Answered Fast.

You already know the part you want. The only real question is where it's cheapest right now — and that shouldn't take twelve browser tabs and a spreadsheet.

Most price-comparison sites match on a product name and hope for the best. We're building this one on a proper spec catalogue — brand, model, MPN, GTIN, even dimensions and weight — so it actually understands what a part is. That means the RTX 4070 you're looking at is matched accurately across every retailer, not confused with a different SKU that happens to share three words in its title. You search by spec, you get the real cheapest price, and you get it in Australian dollars with Australian stock.

// FAQ

Common Questions.

Anything missing? Email support@spencer-the-gathering.com.

When does it launch?
It's in active build right now. There's no fixed public date yet — join the notify list above and you'll get a single email the day it goes live, before it's announced anywhere else.
Will it be free to use?
Yes. Searching for parts and comparing prices is free. You buy from the retailer directly at their price — we're the map, not the shop.
Is it Australia-only?
To start, yes — Australian retailers, Australian stock, prices in AUD including GST. It's built Australian-first because that's the market that's least well served by the existing tools.
How is it different from other comparison sites?
Most match on a product name and hope. Best Price PC Parts is built on a real spec catalogue — brand, model, MPN, GTIN, dimensions — so it knows what a part actually is and matches it accurately across retailers. You search by spec, and the price you're shown is genuinely for the part you meant.
Where does the pricing come from?
Live from retailer product data, refreshed regularly, then normalised against the catalogue so the same part lines up cleanly across every shop. When you click through, you're buying from that retailer at that price.
Do you track me or sell my data?
No. There's no account required to search, and we don't sell personal data to anyone. If you join the notify list, your email is used for exactly one thing — telling you when we launch. See the privacy policy.
