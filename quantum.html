// THE GAME

A Tap Game That Plays Back.

Most tap games are idle clickers in disguise. Quantum Button is the opposite — it's a fast, social, face-to-face fighting game where every tap matters and your enemy can see you sweating.

One button. One opponent. One round. You see their face on screen — they see yours. You're not tapping into a void; you're staring down a real person, trash-talking on the live video feed, dropping power items at the worst possible moment, and watching them rage as the final second ticks down. It's pure social chaos in 60 seconds or less. And once you start, you physically cannot stop.

// QUESTIONS

Things People Ask.

If yours isn't here, hit spencerthegathering@gmail.com.

When is Quantum Button actually launching?
Closed beta is happening early 2026, full public launch later in 2026. Join the waitlist below and you'll be one of the first invited in. We'd rather ship something small that's ready than something big that isn't.
Is the live video feed really private?
Yes — 100%. The video stream goes peer-to-peer over a secure WebRTC connection. There is no server in the middle watching, recording or storing anything. Only the two players in the match ever see each other. When the match ends, the stream is gone.
Do I have to use the video feed?
No. You can turn it off in settings and play with just audio reactions, or fully silent. The face-to-face mode is the signature feature, but the game is fully playable without it.
Will it be free?
Yes. Quantum Button is free to download and free to play. Cosmetics (mythic skins, trails, victory sounds) are the only paid items — and the daily spin crate gives them out for free too. No pay-to-win, ever.
What platforms?
iOS and Android first. Tablet support at launch. Web/desktop is on the longer-term roadmap.
How do power items work — are they unfair?
Both players get the same item pool at the start of every round. You earn them through gameplay, not from spending money. Both players have access to every item. The unfairness is purely in how you use them.
What if I'm not great at tapping fast?
Zen mode is for you — no opponents, no timer, just chilled solo tapping. And Classic mode rewards strategy with power items as much as raw speed. There's a place for everyone.
